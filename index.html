We may disclose data if legally required, for example by a valid order from a competent authority.

5. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (for example to Google LLC in the United States), we rely on an EU adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, submit the following google form [Data Request Form]. We will respond within one month.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion by submitting the following google form [Data Request Form]. Deleting your account removes your Google identifier, VPN configuration and connection data from our systems within 30 days, apart from records we must keep by law. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. You must cancel that in Google Play.

8. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS for the API and WireGuard for the VPN tunnel). Access to our servers is restricted and protected. No system is perfectly secure, but we take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

9. VPN permission

The App uses Android's VpnService permission solely to create the VPN tunnel you request, so that your device's internet traffic is routed through our servers. We do not use this permission to collect personal data or to inject ads or tracking.

10. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete the data.
